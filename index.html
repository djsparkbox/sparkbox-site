Self‑taught. Sought‑after.

Paul Schmidt is a Greenville, SC native with ties to Charleston, SC. As a passion-filled, self-taught turntablist, Paul began sharpening his skills in front of small crowds in the Charleston, SC market. His love for hip hop music directed Paul into producing tracks for local artists. Paul continued to show his promise as a producer, which led him to DJing. His reputation became so esteemed that bookings increased for local parties and venues, to where he developed his stage name DJ Sparkbox — for always showing up with the spark of energy.

DJ Sparkbox has performed alongside acts such as Waka Flocka, Fetty Wap, Sheck Wes, DJ Craze and world-famous Diplo, to name a few. He humbly travels regionally in the southeast while he continues to hold residencies in South Carolina's premier nightclubs. To date, DJ Sparkbox has become one of the most sought-after DJs in South Carolina. His national appearances on DJ City have led him up and down the east coast as well as Toronto. Sparkbox shows no signs of slowing down.

Rooted in hip hop, a Sparkbox set moves through R&B, rap, trap, bass and house, reading the room and keeping the energy up from open to close.

The story

A spark breaking out of the box.

Every DJ starts in a box: the booth, the decks, the set everybody expects. Paul Schmidt taught himself to turn that box into an instrument. Self-taught on the turntables in Charleston, he built a name for always showing up with the spark of energy. That's where DJ Sparkbox comes from.

The brand is that moment when it breaks loose: the blend nobody saw coming, the scratch that cuts through, the room going off. Rooted in hip hop and moving through R&B, rap, trap, bass and house, Sparkbox lives just outside the box.
